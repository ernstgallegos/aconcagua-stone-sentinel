The Climb

Three pressure bands shape every run.

Environmental Pressure

Weather, altitude band, terrain load, and clock state produce compounding EP values each turn.

Body Tolerance

Character profile, fatigue, exposure, resources, and recovery windows define what your body can absorb.

Decision Ethics

Outcome classification rewards timing and safe return, not only upward progress. Retreat is valid when pressure says stop.

Atmospheric States

Silence, geology, and historical memory are treated as first-class context.

Aconcagua is not set dressing. The project anchors itself in route reality, park constraints, and Andean cultural time depth.

Elevation fact

Aconcagua rises to 6,962 m, the highest point in the Americas and outside Asia.

Historical layer

The massif sits within an Andean ceremonial landscape linked to the Qhapaq Ñan network.

Project stance

The game does not romanticize risk. It models uncertainty, consequence, and disciplined return.
