Why this project matters

The project combines documentary mountain framing, narrative design, and systemic gameplay. It privileges observation, judgment, and consequences over speed fantasy.

Environmental authority

The mountain governs

Altitude, weather, and terrain are active systems shaping every turn.

Route logic changes with weather shifts. Pressure escalates through accumulation, not spectacle.

Perception layer

Partial information

Players act through uncertain signals, confidence ranges, and imperfect perception.

Confidence can drop before visible collapse. Timing quality matters as much as action choice.

Outcome ethics

Failure is meaningful

Retreat, pause, and risk recalibration are valid strategic outcomes.

Safe return has explicit narrative weight. The system rewards judgment, not recklessness.

Canonical outcome framing

Success is not binary: the system classifies results based on progression, safety, timing windows, and resource integrity.

Summit and Safe Return

Summit plus safe park exit.

High Point Return

Highest viable altitude reached without final summit closure.

Strategic Retreat / Rescue

Risk-managed retreat or rescue under critical states.

Collapse / Permit / Window
