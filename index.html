Editorial premise

Mountain-first fiction with systemic ethics.

The interface is intentionally slow and breathable. Each block is written to feel like field notes from altitude, where certainty narrows, weather shifts silently, and safe return is a first-class result.

Environmental authority

Altitude, weather, terrain, and time windows dictate terms. Player agency exists inside constraints.

Partial perception

Signals are interpreted through confidence and trend, never omniscient dashboards.

Plural outcomes

Summit is one ending. Strategic retreat and safe return carry equal narrative legitimacy.
