Editorial mountain narrative systems

At altitude, certainty thins out. Every decision becomes a quiet contract with consequence.

Aconcagua: Stone Sentinel is a contemplative mountain narrative about pressure, perception drift, and disciplined restraint. Summit is only one ending; lucid timing and safe return remain the true measure of a run.

Public release: v1.4.7 · Active route: welcome → setup → onboarding → game → outcomes. Built for players, collaborators, artists, festivals, and cultural press.

Design premise

A systemic mountain experience where lucidity matters more than bravado.

This project translates geophysical pressure, partial perception, and ethical decision-making into playable form. The website mirrors that rhythm: less noise, more signal, and clear pathways for deeper exploration.

Environmental authority

Altitude, weather, route timing, and fatigue accumulation drive the run. The mountain sets the terms.

Partial information

Signals are interpreted through confidence ranges, not omniscient readouts. Perception can drift before collapse appears.

Plural outcomes

Success is not only summit. Strategic retreat and safe return are meaningful endings with narrative and systemic weight.

Architecture signal

Built as a coherent EP/BT model, not as cosmetic mountain flavor.

Web-v1 uses a turn resolver where Environmental Pressure and Body Tolerance interact with confidence and trend layers. Contracts, tests, and docs are aligned to keep public claims honest.
