Manifest

The project treats ascent as interpretation, not conquest.

The public site is structured like an expedition notebook: sparse, directional, and evidence-based. No promo clutter, no synthetic urgency.

Geology before spectacle

Terrain, weather windows, and oxygen pressure are first-order systems. Visual style follows that hierarchy.

Time as pressure

Every run is a clock. Delays transform risk, and returning safely has equal narrative authority to summiting.

Altitude ledger

Four bands, four moods, one mountain logic.

2,800–3,400 m Approach Noise of departure fades; pacing discipline starts here.
3,400–4,900 m Acclimatization Small errors compound. Weather and recovery become co-authors.
4,900–6,000 m High camp The mountain narrows your options; certainty thins.
6,000–6,962 m Summit day Momentum means little without timing, reserves, and return margin.
