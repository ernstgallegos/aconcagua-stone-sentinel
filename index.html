The Mountain

Aconcagua is not backdrop. It is the governing body of the experience.

The public site now follows the same ethic as the game: geography first, atmosphere second, mechanics third, promotion last.

“You do not conquer the mountain. You negotiate with weather, fatigue, and time.”

Altitude reference 6,962 m above sea level
Seasonal window Nov 1 → Apr 30
Entry gate Horcones · park-managed permits
Ethos Summit is optional. Return is meaningful.

The design rejects cinematic conquest language. It treats Aconcagua as real terrain with cultural and geological weight, where most critical decisions happen before any summit photo exists.

Every section on this landing is arranged as an expedition narrative: orientation, climb rhythm, atmospheric interpretation, resolver logic, then entry point.

Atmospheric States

Three design layers shape the feeling of altitude.

Thin clarity

High-contrast typography, long leading, and generous negative space keep reading calm under visual pressure.

Cold depth

Layered gradients, restrained texture, and soft lighting produce geological scale without spectacle overload.

Slow movement

Reveal and drift motion are subtle, physics-light, and reduced-motion aware so atmosphere supports readability.
