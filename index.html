Enter the mountain climate

Not a product pitch. A mountain in motion, measured through consequences.

Aconcagua: Stone Sentinel is built as an alpine editorial artifact: altitude, fatigue, weather, and timing are treated as living pressures. Summit is only one possible ending; clear judgment and safe return matter as much as ambition.

The Mountain

Geology, silence, and scale are design material—not backdrop.

The project does not abstract Aconcagua into fantasy terrain. It preserves altitude truth, seasonal windows, route discipline, and cultural weight, then translates those constraints into system behavior.

A place before a game

Aconcagua is the highest peak in the Americas and a provincial park with real rules, permits, and weather boundaries. The website frames that reality first.

An ethic of return

The design rejects summit-only heroics. Retreat, rescue, and disciplined timing remain meaningful outcomes with narrative weight.

The Climb

A run unfolds like an expedition journal, not a power curve.

Progress is deliberate: observe, choose, absorb cost, reassess. What changes most is your interpretation of weak signals under pressure.

01

Approach

Early turns establish rhythm: body-state and weather trend begin to diverge from intention.

02

Compression

At high camp, confidence narrows while timing pressure widens. Resource choices start to define the outcome class.

03

Threshold

Summit-day logic is explicit: late starts, poor acclimatization, and weak weather windows collapse margin fast.

04

Return

Completion is not summit arrival. Runs only resolve fully when park-exit safety is classified.

Atmospheric States

Light, exposure, and perception shift faster than certainty.

Visual cadence mirrors run cadence: dawn promise, amber tension, whiteout ambiguity, blue-hour consequence.

Expedition Logic

The simulation core is explicit, auditable, and data-driven.

Web-v1 resolves each turn through Environmental Pressure and Body Tolerance, then records confidence, trend, and outcome traces for debrief analysis.

Action + time/resource spend
↓
Weather + persistence + stage modifiers
↓
Environmental Pressure ↔ Body Tolerance
↓
Outcome classification + run-log telemetry

Why this matters

  • Public claims can be verified against contracts and tests.
  • Difficulty profiles remain mechanically coherent across UI and runtime.
  • Debrief traces help players learn signal reading, not memorize scripts.
