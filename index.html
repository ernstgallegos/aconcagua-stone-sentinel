Editorial manifesto

The redesign removes promotional noise and restores mountain scale, silence, and consequence.

Instead of generic product blocks, this layout uses asymmetry, large breathing zones, documentary language, and restrained motion. The interface now reflects the same contemplative tension as the game.

Aconcagua is treated as an active system. Light, air, geology, and time pressure shape every line of copy and every navigation decision on this page.

  • Negative space over density

    Fewer simultaneous elements, clearer reading sequence, and less visual fatigue.

  • Editorial typography

    High-contrast serif hierarchy for narrative emphasis, mono labels for systems and telemetry cues.

  • Quiet motion

    Only atmospheric drift and micro-transitions, always disabled under reduced-motion preference.
