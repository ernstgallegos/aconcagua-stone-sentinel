# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to creating a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community for contributors, testers, and readers.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional in all project spaces.
- Assume good intent while giving clear technical feedback.
- Focus critiques on ideas, code, and process (not people).
- Use inclusive language.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Doxxing or publishing private information
- Intimidation, trolling, or sustained hostile behavior
- Spam or disruptive off-topic conduct

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this policy.
