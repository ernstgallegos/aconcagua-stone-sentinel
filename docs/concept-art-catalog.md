# Concept Art Catalog — Visual Analysis

This document catalogs the visual assets in `art/concept-art/curated/ig/` and `art/cover/ig/`, providing thematic analysis, palette notes, mood descriptors, and recommended use cases for each image.

Images are organized by folder and numbered sequence. The two folders alternate in tone: `ig/` odd-numbered files (concept-art) are primarily illustrated/painterly; even-numbered files (cover) include both photorealistic and illustrated treatments.

---

## Folder: `art/concept-art/curated/ig/`

Illustrated concept pieces. Textured digital painting / watercolor aesthetic. Subject: the relationship between the human figure and the mountain.

---

### `1.png` — Climber Before the Summit (Dawn)

**Style:** Digital illustration, watercolor grain texture  
**Subject:** Lone climber (orange jacket, red cap, trekking pole) viewed from behind. Standing on rocky, arid plateau.  
**Mountain:** Aconcagua centered and dominant, full pyramid silhouette, snow-capped. Layered ridgelines recede into atmospheric distance.  
**Light:** Dawn or late dusk. Warm amber and coral light from below the horizon. Sky and mountain suffused in dusty rose and lavender.  
**Mood:** Contemplation. Quiet resolve. The mountain is immense; the figure is grounded, not overwhelmed.  
**Palette:** Dusty rose · lavender · warm amber · burnt coral · pale ice at summit  
**Design alignment:** Embodies core pillar — "the mountain dominates the human figure" — without drama. Scale is visible but not theatrical.  
**Recommended use:** Title screen hero, expedition setup background, landing page primary visual, cover art. Strong as a standalone image at portrait ratio.

### `3.png` — Climber at the Ridge (Atmospheric Haze)

**Style:** Digital illustration, finely textured painterly surface  
**Subject:** Lone climber (orange jacket, blue hood, trekking pole) from behind. Standing at the edge of a rocky ridge.  
**Mountain:** Aconcagua partially obscured by atmospheric haze and layered fog. Summit visible but diffuse.  
**Light:** Twilight or heavy dusk. Diffuse, non-directional light. No sharp shadows.  
**Mood:** Uncertainty. The route is there but not fully readable. The climber is paused, considering.  
**Palette:** Deep mauve · blue-grey · purple shadow · warm orange accent on figure  
**Design alignment:** Communicates "ambiguous routes and decision-making without explicit guidance." The haze is the environmental pressure — present, not dramatic.  
**Recommended use:** Mid-game atmospheric screen, weather-window decision moments, approach sections. Excellent for uncertainty/risk states in UI.

---

### `5.png` — The Column (High-Altitude Ascent)

**Style:** Digital illustration, soft atmospheric/painterly treatment  
**Subject:** Seven climbers in a single file ascending a steep snow slope. Varying colored jackets (orange, red, green, blue). All carrying packs and poles.  
**Mountain:** Summit visible at upper center, partially shrouded in storm or cloud.  
**Light:** Overcast. Diffuse white-grey light. No directional shadow. Storm-flat.  
**Mood:** Shared effort. Exposed. The pace is deliberate. The mountain ahead remains indifferent.  
**Palette:** Near-white · lavender-blush · warm amber and teal accents on figures · grey-blue shadow on snow  
**Design alignment:** Rare group image — communicates that multiple players/characters face the same conditions. The expedition as system, not individual heroism.  
**Recommended use:** Onboarding screens, expedition-setup context, social/multiplayer framing. Also effective as a background for group or scenario cards.

Cover images. Mixed styles: painterly watercolor + photorealistic with artistic texture overlay. Subject: the mountain as landscape, rarely populated.

### `2.png` — High-Altitude Panorama (Sunrise)

**Style:** Photorealistic with painterly texture overlay (grain + color grading)  
**Subject:** No human figures. Rocky moraine foreground with scattered snow. Andean peaks in middle distance. Vast sky.  
**Mountain:** Multiple Andean summits receding in atmospheric haze. The dominant summit (likely Cerro Mercedario or a sub-peak) partially snow-covered.  
**Light:** Sunrise or sunset. Dramatic warm gradient — deep orange at horizon fading through pink to cold blue above. Dynamic cloud cover lit from below.  
**Mood:** Vast. Exposed. Raw altitude. The sky is as active as the terrain.  
**Palette:** Burnt orange · rose-gold · cold grey-blue · ochre rock · ice white · coral cloud  
**Design alignment:** "Climate as a persistent condition rather than a dramatic event." This image is the exception — the sky here IS the drama. Appropriate for summit-day moments.  
**Recommended use:** Summit or near-summit game moments, high environmental pressure state, cover/social media thumbnail. The warmth of the sky contrasts well against the cold design system tokens.

---

### `4.png` — Aconcagua from the Valley (Approach, Clear Day)

**Style:** Watercolor illustration  
**Subject:** No human figures. Wide valley approach. Dry scrubland and rock formations in foreground. Aconcagua in full view centered.  
**Mountain:** Classic full silhouette — broad base, layered ridge lines, snow-capped summit. Archetypal "first sighting" composition.  
**Light:** Daytime, clear sky. Even light, no strong directional shadow. Blue sky with subtle texture.  
**Mood:** Monumental but calm. This is the mountain as a system — you can see all of it, understand its scale, begin to plan.  
**Palette:** Ochre · raw umber · warm sand · grey-blue sky · pure white summit  
**Design alignment:** "Scale and solitude." No climber in frame — the human reference is the viewer. Communicates route ambiguity through visual complexity of the ridgeline.  
**Recommended use:** Landing page section background, scenario selection screen, documentation and whitepaper imagery. Strong at wide/landscape ratio.

## Visual language observations

### Palette consistency
The confirmed images share a warm-cool tension: amber/coral/orange climber gear against lavender/mauve/grey-blue mountain environments. This mirrors the project's design-system accent contrast: `--accent-amber #d9b996` against cold `--bg-*` surfaces.

### Figure treatment
When human figures appear, they are always viewed **from behind** (images 1, 3) or at a distance that anonymizes them (image 5). This is intentional: the player projects onto the climber. The figure is a vector, not a character portrait.

### Scale grammar
In every figure-present image, the human occupies the bottom quarter of the frame. The mountain occupies the top half or more. This is non-negotiable visual grammar for the project.

### Style split
- `concept-art/ig/` (illustrated): warmer, more intimate, accessible. Suitable for in-prototype screens.
- `cover/ig/` (mixed): more documentary weight. Suitable for landing page, public-facing surfaces, social media.
