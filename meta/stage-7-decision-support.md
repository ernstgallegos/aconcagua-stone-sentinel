# Stage 7 Decision Support — Public Playtest Evaluation

This document is a decision-support artifact for non-code teams.

## Objective

Decide whether to continue public prototype iteration as-is, narrow scope, or open the next commercialization/funding track.

## Evaluation criteria

### Product evidence
- Outcome distribution remains mechanically coherent (no structural dead-end outcomes).
- Public players can complete full Part 1 loop without onboarding dead-ends.
- Debrief output yields actionable next-run guidance.

### Operational evidence
- Release-readiness gates pass with explicit command evidence.
- No contradictory public status/version statements across README, roadmap, press-kit, and repo-truth docs.

### Audience evidence
- Qualitative feedback indicates clarity of core promise: “mountain-first decision game; summit is not the only success”.
- Users understand current Part 2 scope as narrative bridge preview (not full Part 2 gameplay).

## Suggested metrics

### Quantitative (weekly)
- Completion rate from title to any terminal outcome.
- Share of outcomes by category (safe return / strategic retreat / collapse/resource/permit).
- Drop-off points by screen (title, setup, onboarding, game turn 1–5).

### Qualitative (weekly)
- Top 5 friction comments from player feedback.
- Top 5 “clarity wins” comments (what players understood immediately).
- Confusion rate for Part 2 scope wording.

## Decision tracks

1. **Iterate public prototype only** (short cycle UX/polish).
2. **Prepare funding conversations** (use whitepaper + validated metrics package).
3. **Prepare commercialization discovery** (platform, scope, staffing, timeline).

## Priority feedback channels

1. Structured playtest form (short + mandatory core questions).
2. Email feedback channel.
3. Instagram/community comments (triaged weekly into product issues).

## Ownership suggestion

- Product/Design: criteria interpretation and UX prioritization.
- Engineering: runtime quality gates and defect trend.
- Marketing/Comms: messaging clarity and channel response quality.
- Strategy/Production: go/no-go recommendation and resource planning.
