# Devlog 004 — Gameplay Densification

The fourth iteration of `web-v1` focuses on density, not breadth.

The design objective remains unchanged: a mountain-system where decisions are constrained by physiology, environment, and uncertainty. This update increases the texture of those constraints without introducing progression economies or combat abstractions.

## What changed

- Acclimatization was added as a first-class state variable with gain, decay, and stage thresholds.
- Summit attempts now interact with temporal windows rather than a flat stage penalty.
- An emergency-pack pre-departure choice was introduced to externalize contingency planning.
- `Observe` was added as a low-cost non-movement action to privilege information gathering.
- Ambient diegetic signaling was added between status and narrative layers.
- The route schematic was rebuilt as a dynamic SVG to improve legibility while preserving austerity.
- Debrief now includes compact analytics for retrospective reading of decisions.

## Why this matters

The previous version validated loop coherence. This version tests whether coherence survives higher decision density.

The mountain remains the authority. The body remains the bottleneck. Time remains non-negotiable.

## What did not change

- No XP systems
- No leveling loops
- No loot abstraction
- No skill-tree progression
- No action-combat layer

The project still aims at reflective decision pressure, not accumulation.
