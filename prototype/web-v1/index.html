      The summit was reached.
      The mountain was respected.

      What comes next puts everything you learned here to a different test. Not a simulation. The real expedition — with the full group, the real terrain, and no guaranteed outcomes. The mountain will decide.

      Part 2 · Expedition Guides

      Stone Sentinel Expeditions — The guides

      The expedition is led by two EPGAMT-certified guides from Mendoza.

      Agustina Villanueva — Lead guide. Twelve seasons on Aconcagua, eight as lead. Certified UIAA mountaineering guide since 27. Direct, economical with words, and precise about conditions. When she says it's time to descend, the discussion ends.

      Alejandro Molina — Assistant guide. Second season. Strong climber, fast learner, tends toward optimism when conditions are uncertain. The group notices the mentor dynamic between them from day one.

      Their operating principle, stated plainly at the hotel briefing: no summit is worth compromising a safe return.
